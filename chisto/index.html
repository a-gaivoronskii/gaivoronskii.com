Do I need Apple Health?

No. The cleaning timer, manual logging, reminders, styles, and progress work without Health access. Connect Health if you want workout tracking and choose to save sessions there.

Are all styles included?

Fresh Linen, Quiet Wood, and Studio Loft are included. Additional styles are available as optional one-time purchases.

Which devices does Chisto support?

The upcoming iPhone release requires iOS 27 or later. The companion Watch app requires watchOS 27 or later.
