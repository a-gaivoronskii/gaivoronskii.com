5. HealthKit Integration

The App integrates with Apple HealthKit to record workout sessions and heart rate during cleaning. Active calories shown by the App and saved to Apple Health are estimates based on selected cleaning types, active duration, and your chosen weight. You may enter weight manually, explicitly choose to read the latest available Body Mass sample from Apple Health, or skip setup and use a generic 70 kg reference. These calculations are not measurements of your actual energy expenditure. Each new session keeps its recorded weight input even if your weight changes later. Older sessions without a stored input use the generic reference. During a live session, the App shows a provisional average intensity that is recalculated when you select cleaning activities. For a manually entered cleaning session, the App can save the estimate as user-entered active energy only after you explicitly choose to do so. Entering weight in chisto does not write weight to Apple Health. You can revoke HealthKit permissions through your device's Settings.
