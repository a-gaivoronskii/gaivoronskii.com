Privacy at Smeta

Your data stays
under your control.

Smeta is designed around private, on-device financial tracking. Starting with iOS version 1.3, transaction files are processed on your iPhone.

No selling No ad tracking You choose what leaves your device

Overview

Smeta is built to help you track income, spending, accounts, and balances. Your financial data should stay under your control.

Data You Add

The app stores the accounts, transactions, categories, names, notes, currencies, settings, and exchange rates you add on your device to provide balances, reports, search, import, and export.

iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is optional. When it is enabled, your app data is synced through your private iCloud account so it can be available on your devices. You can turn iCloud sync off in Settings at any time.

Google Drive Sync

Google Drive sync is optional. If you connect it, Smeta requests your Google account email and access to its private app-data folder in Drive. Smeta uses the email to identify your account and encrypts the records it syncs on your device before uploading them. Your recovery code stays with you.

Disconnecting stops sync and revokes access. To remove the encrypted data from Drive, use Settings › Google Drive › Delete Google Drive data in Smeta. This keeps your local records and any private iCloud data.

Import and Export

In iOS version 1.3 and later, Smeta parses transaction files on your iPhone. Smeta stores imported transactions, source file names, file hashes, and import details to detect duplicates and let you undo an import. Saved column mappings stay on your device until you delete them in Import Parsing settings.

Exported CSV files contain the data you choose to export. Protect them and share them only with people or services you trust.

Remote Import in Earlier iOS Versions

Version 1.2 and earlier iOS releases that offered Remote AI Import could send generated column labels and a structural pattern from up to 12 rows when you enabled the feature and local parsing needed help. These requests went directly to Qwen (Alibaba Cloud), Kimi (Moonshot AI), or Perplexity for column suggestions. They contained patterns such as empty cells, numeric signs, and repeated text. Raw headers and transaction values stayed on your device.

A provider may retain past requests and responses under its own terms and privacy policy. On an older version that offers the feature, turn it off in Settings › AI Import › Turn Off Remote AI Import to stop future requests.

Protection and Retention

User-added accounts and associated transactions remain on your device until you delete them in the app. If iCloud Sync is enabled, you can delete Smeta’s private iCloud data by choosing Settings › iCloud › Turn Off and Delete iCloud Data. Contact Support for questions about available deletion options.

Your Choices and Deletion

You can delete an account and its associated transactions in Settings › Accounts. For questions or requests about your privacy rights or deletion options, choose Settings › Contact Support or email andrei@gaivoronskii.com. Do not include transaction details.

No Selling or Tracking

Smeta does not sell your data, does not use advertising tracking, and does not collect your transactions for marketing. When you update exchange rates, the app may request public rates from an exchange-rate provider using currency codes only.

Updates

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app. Continuing to use Smeta after an update means the new version applies.
